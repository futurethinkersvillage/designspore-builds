White Rock Equity · round three · monograms

The WR monogram.

Eight luxury monograms. The ideas came from AI concepts generated on your local models (shown at the bottom). Each mark was then rebuilt from real serif letterforms, with the weave cut as clean shapes. Beside each: on the brand red, on dark, and the icon at app and favicon size. Earlier rounds: two, one.

Where the ideas came from

Concepts from AI Studio (FLUX.2 klein and Z-Image Turbo) for four prompts: couture ligature, foil-stamped card, fine-line ring and carved marble. The run was cut short when the GPU was shared with a RealityScan job, so these are the six that finished. None is used as artwork; the monograms above are drawn from real type.
