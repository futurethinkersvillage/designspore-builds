03The Missing Layer

Festivals make a people

Your definition of a network state asks for a sense of national consciousness and a capacity for collective action.

Conferences can’t make either. People become a people the way they always have — by building something together, and gathering around it.

The largest popup city on earth has the ritual, but no economy and no permanence. Network School has the economy. Put them together and you get both.

04The Popup

NS North: a summer popup

Three weeks in the mountains of British Columbia, summer 2027 — a build sprint, not a vacation — ending in an art festival the whole region comes to.

Week 1 · Build

Raise the village

Members, local artists and our makerspace build the art and the camp — CNC, LED, zome raising.

Week 2 · Learn & burn

Talks by day, trails at dawn

Talks, hackathons, proctored maker credentials. Mornings on trails, lake, sauna and cold plunge.

Week 3 · Ship

Demo day & film night

Demos at the amphitheatre. An AI-film night on the drive-in screen. Art, ventures and films shipped.

Finale · Light

The festival

A three-day art & music festival, open to the region — with free local days.

05Why A Festival

Why members would choose it

01

Bonds, not badges

You don’t bond in an audience. You bond building a camp together at 2 a.m. That’s collective action, practiced.

02

A rehearsal for a state

Infrastructure, logistics, rules and a census — a city in miniature, stood up and run every year.

03

Output, not talk

Art, structures, prototypes and films shipped. Everyone leaves having built something real.

04

The people you’re missing

Artists, makers, families and North Americans — talent that never shows up to a crypto conference.

Mountain trails, a private lake, sauna and cold plunge. Proof-of-workout with a view.

06

Lore

The stories and images people retell for years. A society runs on them — and they travel further than any keynote.

A conference is where you hear the future. A popup is where you build it.

Beauty is a moat

It’s hard to be the person who shuts down the place that funds local artists and makes the valley more beautiful.

A node needs a local constituency before it ever needs a permit. Art builds one faster than anything else — and it’s the part of your culture outsiders can see.

09Maker Culture

We build the nodes

Your roadmap calls for prefab nodes — “a portable template that we can open source and recreate anywhere.”

We’re building that template now: zome villages cut on our own CNC, raised by small crews in weeks.

  • Two formats — 9m Residence (bath + kitchen) and 6m Glamping, ~$85K and ~$42K all-in
  • Design to cut list in a browser — our Dome & Zome Generator and WikiHouse configurator
  • Modular and nature-integrated — the kind of site you’re exploring in Burabay, prototyped here
