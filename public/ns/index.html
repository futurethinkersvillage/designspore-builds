02The Thesis

Infrastructure for a post-AI world

As AI takes over more knowledge work, what becomes scarce is real capability — people who can build, adapt and look after each other.

That isn’t learned on a screen or in a city apartment. It needs land, tools, and a community that does things together.

We’ve spent over ten years researching and refining this thesis through Future Thinkers — and the last five building it on the ground.

03The Approach

Frontier hands, AI-native minds

The people who do well next can do both: build and fix things in the real world, and work fluently with AI. Almost nowhere teaches the two together.

Frontier hands

Building, growing food, fixing what breaks, being at home outdoors. Practical skills that make a person capable and calm.

AI-native minds

AI tools, code, design, digital fabrication, systems thinking and learning how to learn. Tools that multiply what one person can do.

04The Gap

Nothing combines it all

ResortsComfort and services — built for short, passive stays.
Retreats & festivalsPowerful experiences — then everyone goes home.
Co-livingShared space and community — urban, transient, rarely for families.
Schools & universitiesCredentials — with few real-world skills and little time outdoors.
MakerspacesTools and know-how — but no place to live and no land to build on.
EcovillagesA holistic vision — without professional operations or repeatability.
Portal.Place puts land, making, learning and community in one place — run by people who’ve operated it for years.

08What’s Missing

Today we close for winter

The land, the team, the experience and the audience are in place. What we don’t have yet is the infrastructure to host people for longer, all year.

A seasonal operationWe run May to October today.
No long-stay lodgingRV sites and bunk cabins work for a week, not for a season or a cohort.
No shared work spaceNo co-working or dedicated learning space yet.
Utilities sized for todaySeptic, power and water need to be sized up for more people, year-round.
