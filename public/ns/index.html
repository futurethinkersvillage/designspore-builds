02The Thesis

A cultural living & learning campus
and a prototype lab

AI is rewriting how we work, learn and live — faster than institutions can adapt. Small, tech-empowered communities can now build what once took whole institutions.

Portal.Place is building a campus where people live, learn, make and build together — and a prototype lab for the tools, spaces and culture of what comes next.

Not a resort, a retreat, co-living, a school or a makerspace — all of them, on one campus. Grounded in over ten years of research through Future Thinkers.

09The Vision

Not just a place — a beacon

Our vision is a campus that shows what living, learning and building can look like in a post-AI world — a place others point to, visit and learn from.

Wells Gray would be the first campus in a planned network of cultural living & learning campuses — some in nature, some in cities, working with other communities and campuses.

It’s grounded in over ten years of research through Future Thinkers, and six years of operating on the land.

What partners get
Host your peoplePop-ups, residencies and team-building retreats on the land.
Media partnershipFuture Thinkers and our production team tell the story — yours included.
For a lead partner: co-design the campusFor the right partner, such as Network School, a real say in how the campus is built and where it goes.
It starts with one campus — then expands into a network.
