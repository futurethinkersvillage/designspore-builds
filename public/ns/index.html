02The Thesis

A living & learning campus
for the AI era

AI, remote work, digital nomadism and alternative education are rewriting how we work, learn and live — faster than places can adapt. Small, tech-empowered communities can now build what once took whole institutions.

Portal.Place is building a campus where people live, learn, create and build together — a culture lab for what comes next.

More than a resort, retreat, co-living, school or makerspace — combining elements of all in one campus. Grounded in over ten years of research through Future Thinkers.

08The Lab

Tech we build

A culture lab needs its own tools — so we build software and AI tools to run the campus and design what gets made on it.

In developmentVillage Stack

An operating system for the campus: operations, work-stays, members, events, energy and AI agents in one place.

LiveDome & Zome Generator

Design geodesic domes and zomes in 3D, with live cut lists for our CNC shop.

LiveDigital Twin

A living map of all 400 acres: every site, utility, trail and phase of the plan, in layers.

PrototypeLandQuest

An offline NFC and GPS treasure-hunt app for the land, with 3D-printed tokens hidden on the trails.

AI + a digital twin: what it unlocks for land
Plan before you buildTest layouts, utilities and phases before breaking ground.
Run the landBookings, maintenance, energy and water in one live view.
Ask the land“Where can ten cabins go near power and water?”
Share the visionInteractive tours for partners, members and councils.

10Why Canada

Canada has space, water, clean power and deep AI talent

A country with room to grow, turning back toward building. For anyone thinking in decades, this is a frontier.

2ndlargest country on Earth, with about 4 people per km²
~20%of the world’s surface fresh water, and 9% of its forests
98%clean power in BC, at some of North America’s lowest rates
500 MWof hydro-powered AI compute planned for BC, starting in Kamloops
2017the world’s first national AI strategy; home of Hinton and Bengio
Top 5least climate-vulnerable nations (ND-GAIN)

And our corner of it: surrounded by Wells Gray — 5,400 km² of protected wilderness and 41 named waterfalls.

Sources: Statistics Canada, NRCan, BC Hydro, Bell (2025), Government of Canada, ND-GAIN (2023), BC Parks
