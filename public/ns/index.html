02The Thesis

A prototype lab for what comes next

AI is rewriting how we work, learn and live — faster than institutions can adapt. Small, tech-empowered communities can now build what once took whole institutions.

For over a decade on Future Thinkers, we’ve explored that frontier: technology, culture, meta-skills and new ways of living together. Portal.Place is where we build it for real.

A campus rich in culture, creativity and experimentation — where founders, makers, artists and families build cool things for what comes next.

03The Approach

Frontier hands, AI-native minds

The people who do well next can do both: build and fix things in the real world, and work fluently with AI. Almost nowhere teaches the two together.

Frontier hands

Building, fixing what breaks, making things that last, being at home outdoors. Practical skills that make people capable, resourceful and resilient.

AI-native minds

AI tools, code, design, digital fabrication, systems thinking and learning how to learn. Tools that multiply what one person can do.

07Why Canada

Room to build

Space, water, clean power and deep AI talent — in a country turning back toward building. For anyone thinking in decades, this is a frontier.

2ndlargest country on Earth, with about 4 people per km²
~20%of the world’s surface fresh water, and 9% of its forests
98%clean power in BC, at some of North America’s lowest rates
500 MWof hydro-powered AI compute planned for BC, starting in Kamloops
2017the world’s first national AI strategy; home of Hinton and Bengio
Top 5least climate-vulnerable nations (ND-GAIN)

And our corner of it: surrounded by Wells Gray — 5,400 km² of protected wilderness and 41 named waterfalls.

Sources: Statistics Canada, NRCan, BC Hydro, Bell (2025), Government of Canada, ND-GAIN (2023), BC Parks

15The Vision

Not just a place — a network

Wells Gray is the first campus in a planned network of cultural living & learning campuses.

Not all will be in nature — some will be in cities. Each takes its own shape; all share a culture of making, learning and building together.

We want to collaborate with other communities and campuses — sharing people, programs and what we learn. Over time, we’ll open up our tools and templates too.

What partners get
Host your peoplePop-ups, residencies and team-building retreats on the land.
Media partnershipFuture Thinkers and our production team tell the story — yours included.
For a lead partner: co-design the campusFor the right partner, such as Network School, a real say in how the campus is built and where it goes.
It starts with one campus — then expands into a network.
