05The Gap

Why existing models don’t meet the need

No existing model combines all the elements in a repeatable way.

Resorts provide seasonal comfort and services, but are designed for short stays and passive consumption.

Retreats & festivals provide cultural and learning experiences, but don’t offer a viable long-term lifestyle.

RV parks, glamping, and campsites offer access to nature and basic amenities, but no cultural continuity.

Co-living / co-working facilities provide shared infrastructure and community lifestyle, but are typically urban, transient, and not suitable for families.

Wellness membership clubs give access to health amenities, but are visit-based and not lifestyle-integrated.

Intentional communities / ecovillages explore holistic lifestyle alternatives, but typically lack professional operations, capital discipline, and repeatability.

Each one solves one piece of the puzzle. None offers a systemic approach.

06Our Solution

A new asset class

Portal.Place is a new category that combines real estate, membership, and emerging technology. It’s a membership-based village campus and future network designed for:

  • Monthly, seasonal, and annual subscription access for stays that combine lifestyle and learning
  • Shared co-working, wellness, event, and maker infrastructure
  • Defined culture, seasonal rhythms, and learning programs that drive recurring visits
  • Professional, tech-enabled operations and repeatable lifestyle standards
  • A membership and operating system that allows onboarding other destinations — unlocking scalability and shared economics, and turning the platform into a real-world-asset-backed network

We are building a repeatable operating model for lifestyle-first living in the post-AI world.
