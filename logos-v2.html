White Rock Equity · logo directions, round two

Ten marks, set in stone.

No illustrations this time. Most of these are type-led, as serious investment firms' logos are, with the rock carried by one deliberate detail. Beside each: on the brand red, on dark, and the icon at app and favicon size (32px, 16px). Round one is still here for reference.
